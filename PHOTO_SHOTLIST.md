# Photo Shot List — The Backyard Fig

> All photos should be Randall's own originals.
> Upload finished photos to the `/photos/` folder in this repo, named with the chapter
> number and a short description (e.g., `ch01-grandmothers-tree.jpg`).
>
> **Target camera settings for book-quality photos:**
> - Shoot in the best light you have — early morning or late afternoon (golden hour) is ideal
> - Avoid harsh midday shadows on fruit or leaves
> - Shoot in RAW or highest-quality JPEG your phone/camera offers
> - Landscape orientation preferred for most shots; portrait for close-ups
> - Keep backgrounds clean — aim a shot at the sky or a green hedge rather than a cluttered background
> - Minimum 2400px wide for print quality; 3000–4000px preferred
