> **How to use:** Copy this file, rename it `ch0X-dossier.md`, and fill it out completely
> before any prose is drafted for that chapter. Present it to Randall for approval. No
> drafting begins until the dossier is approved.
>
> **AI prompt to generate a first-pass dossier:**
> Load: `PROJECT.md` + `STYLE_GUIDE.md` + `research/randall-voice-profile.md` +
> `research/terminology.md` + `research/repetition-log.md` + the relevant research file(s)
> + `CHAPTER_LOG.md` (for outline reference).
> Then say: *"Build a chapter dossier for Chapter X using this template. Flag any research
> gaps. Do not draft prose yet."*
>
> Randall reviews, revises, and approves the dossier. Then drafting begins.
