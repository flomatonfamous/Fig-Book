# YouTube Transcript Ingest Rules (LOCKED — June 4, 2026)

> Randall approved these rules for how transcripts are used in *The Backyard Fig*.
> The ghostwriter must follow this hierarchy on every chapter.

---

## Source Hierarchy (when sources conflict)

| Priority | Source | Use for |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **1** | Locked `PROJECT.md` decisions | Canon — never override |
| **2** | Verified research notes (`research/*.md`, extension guides, `[VERIFY]` cleared) | **Facts** in manuscript |
| **3** | Randall's approved manuscript text (Ch. 1, etc.) | Voice + confirmed personal facts |
| **4** | YouTube transcripts (`Youtube Transcripts/`) | **Voice, stories, anecdotes, candidate material** — NOT automatic facts |
| **5** | Randall conversation / explicit approval | Confirms anything before it prints as fact |

**When a transcript conflicts with research or verified data: research wins.**
Do not treat Randall's spoken words in a video as fact until confirmed or consistent with research.

---

## What Transcripts ARE For

- **Voice and personality** — how Randall talks, jokes, hedges, tells stories
- **Personal story material** — orchard moments, failures, family, dogs, customers, community
- **Chapter openings and closings** — farm candidate paragraphs when a slot needs Randall's voice
- **Sensory detail** — how he describes ripeness, flavor, the greenhouse, the orchard walk
- **Community color** — FigBid, gatherings, trades, naming confusion (as his experience, not gospel)

---

## What Transcripts Are NOT For

- **Botanical or historical facts** unless they match `research/` notes
- **Variety claims** (best fig, cold hardy, San Pedro type) without cross-check to `research/fig-varieties.md` and research notes
- **Statistics or counts** spoken on camera (142 varieties, 94% success, zone numbers) — verify or use as "in my orchard" experience only
- **Names and spellings** from auto-transcription — verify before manuscript (many garbled in `.txt`)
- **Direct quotes** — synthesize into original prose; never paste transcript lines into the book

---

## Ingest Workflow (every time new transcripts are added)

1. **Store raw files** in `Youtube Transcripts/` — do not edit Randall's uploads.
2. **Log the file** in `research/youtube-transcript-index.md` (filename, date, topic if known).
3. **Extract to two places:**
   - `research/youtube-story-bank.md` — stories, openings, personality, chapter slots
   - `research/youtube-content-notes.md` — topic notes tagged **STORY**, **VOICE**, or **FACT-CANDIDATE** (needs verify)
4. **Flag conflicts** in `research/youtube-content-notes.md` under "Conflicts with research" when transcript says something research contradicts.
5. **Update** `research/randall-voice-profile.md` only when new speech patterns appear — not for unverified facts.
6. **Never auto-insert** story-bank items into manuscript. Use as drafts for Randall to approve or edit.

---

## Chapter Open / Close — Personal Story Slots

When a chapter needs a personal opening or closing:

1. Check `research/youtube-story-bank.md` for tagged candidates for that chapter.
2. Synthesize 2–4 sentences in Randall's voice from **story-bank** material only.
3. Mark draft as `[DRAFT FROM TRANSCRIPT — Randall review]` if not yet approved.
4. If no good candidate exists, leave `[RANDALL'S OPENING: topic]` and list story-bank options in session notes.

Randall may replace, shorten, or rewrite any drafted opening. His edits become the new voice reference.

---

## Tagging Convention (story bank and content notes)

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| `[STORY]` | Personal anecdote — safe for narrative once Randall okays tone |
| `[VOICE]` | Phrasing or habit — use for style, not content |
| `[EXPERIENCE]` | "In my orchard…" — Randall's experience, not universal fact |
| `[FACT-CANDIDATE]` | Spoken claim — verify against research before any factual use |
| `[CONFLICT]` | Transcript disagrees with research — do not use as fact |
| `[CH.#-OPEN]` / `[CH.#-CLOSE]` | Candidate for chapter opening or closing |

---

## Examples

**OK (story from transcript):**
> I tried waxing both ends of a cutting because a viewer suggested it. Five out of seventy-five rooted. I don't recommend it.

**NOT OK (transcript as fact without verify):**
> Figs are reliably hardy to Zone 6. *(If research says Zone 7 — research wins.)*

**OK (experience, not universal):**
> In my orchard, LSU Purple took a couple of years before the fruit really turned good.

**NOT OK (variety gospel from video):**
> Black Madeira is the number one fig in the world. *(Subjective on-camera opinion — not a book fact.)*
