# Terminology — The Backyard Fig

> **Purpose:** Canonical usage for every term that could drift across 13 chapters written
> months apart. When a term appears here, this is how it is used in this book — no exceptions
> without a deliberate locked decision. Prevents the same concept being called three different
> things in three different chapters.
>
> **How to use:** Before drafting any section, check this file for any technical term you
> plan to use. If a term is not listed, add it here before it goes into manuscript.

---

## Plant Biology & Anatomy

| Term | Preferred Usage | Avoid | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|-------|
| **syconium** | "syconium" — the inverted hollow receptacle that is the fig fruit | "false fruit," "multiple fruit," "fig structure" | Explain on first use in Ch. 2; do not re-explain in later chapters |
| **ostiole** | "ostiole" or "the eye" (colloquially) | "opening," "hole," "tip opening" | "Eye" is acceptable in casual grower context; "ostiole" for technical context |
| **fig honey** | "fig honey" | "syrup," "nectar drop," "bead" | Community term; lowercase, no quotes after first use |
| **breba crop** | "breba crop" or "breba" | "first crop," "early crop," "spring crop" | Always lowercase; never hyphenated |
| **main crop** | "main crop" | "second crop," "summer crop," "primary crop" | Always lowercase |
| **parthenocarpic** | "parthenocarpic" | "self-pollinating" (inaccurate — they don't pollinate, they fruit without it) | Explain on first use; do not use "self-fertile" as a synonym here |
| **caprifig** | "caprifig" | "male fig," "wild fig," "pollen fig" | One word, lowercase |
| **Ficus carica** | Italicized on first use in any chapter; plain text after | Do not italicize repeatedly | Spell out on first use in Ch. 2; not required in every chapter |
| **latex** | "latex" or "milky sap" | "milk," "juice," "white stuff" | "Milky sap" acceptable in casual context; "latex" in technical context |
| **FigCrack** | "FigCrack" (capitalized, one word) | "fig crack," "skin split," "cracking" | Community term — treat as proper noun |
| **fig honey** | lowercase after first introduction | "Fig Honey" | Community term, not a product name |

## Growing Conditions & Zones

| Term | Preferred Usage | Avoid | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|-------|
| **Zone 7 / Zone 8 / Zone 9** | "Zone 7," "Zone 8" (capitalized, no quotes) | "zone 7," "hardiness zone 7," "USDA Zone 7" | First use in book may include "USDA hardiness" but drop it after that |
| **dormancy / dormant** | "dormant period," "go dormant" | "hibernation," "sleep," "rest period" | Figs are deciduous, not tropical — dormancy is biologically accurate |
| **Gulf South / Gulf Coast** | "Gulf South" or "Gulf Coast" | "Deep South," "lower South" (inconsistent) | Use "Gulf South" for climate context; "Gulf Coast" for geographic reference |
| **drip line** | "drip line" | "canopy edge," "edge of the canopy" | Standard horticultural term — use consistently |
| **root zone** | "root zone" | "root area," "base of the tree" | Use when referring to the area not to be disturbed |
