LSU Thibodaux (Late Black), LSU DC-6, LSU St. Gabriel

> These are Southern-bred varieties developed for Gulf Coast conditions — heat tolerance,
> humidity resistance, and reliable production in Alabama and similar climates.

Col De Dame Roja, Col De Dame Grise, Col De Dame Ciutat

## Notes for Chapter Drafting

### Chapter 4 — Flavor Profiles
Before drafting the flavor profiles section, ask Randall to share his personal tasting
notes on at least 10–15 of his favorites. The following are strong candidates to feature
prominently based on community reputation and variety prominence:

- **Black Madeira** — widely considered among the finest figs in the world by collectors
- **Col De Dame Noir / Blanc** — prestige varieties with exceptional flavor reputation
- **Violet De Bordeaux (VDB)** — classic, one of the most popular in the US market
- **LSU Purple** — Southern staple, widely grown and well-known
- **Peters Honey** — notable honey flavor, well-known variety
- **Creme Di Fragola** — the name means "strawberry cream" — flavor profile is notable
- **Hardy Chicago** — cold-hardy standout, important for northern readers
- **California Brown Turkey** — the most widely available fig in the US
- **Green Ischia** — one of the oldest named varieties in cultivation
- **White Marselles / Lattarulla** — bright, mild, classic
