# Source Ledger — The Backyard Fig

> **Purpose:** Every factual claim that goes into the manuscript gets logged here before the
> chapter is locked. This prevents unverified information from slipping through and gives
> a fast reference for the Appendix sources section.
>
> **Confidence levels:**
> - **High** — verified from university extension, peer-reviewed, or Randall's direct documented experience
> - **Medium** — widely accepted growing knowledge, community consensus, plausible but not formally cited
> - **Low** — unverified, uncertain, or second-hand — must be resolved before chapter is finalized
>
> **Rule:** Any "Low" confidence claim must be flagged with `[VERIFY]` in the manuscript draft
> and cannot remain in a locked chapter without being upgraded or removed.
