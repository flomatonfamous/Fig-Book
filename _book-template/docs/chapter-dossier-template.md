# Chapter Dossier Template

> **How to use:** Copy this file, rename it `ch0X-dossier.md` inside `manuscript/` or a
> chapter subfolder, and fill it out completely before any prose is drafted for that chapter.
> Present it to the author for approval. No drafting begins until the dossier is approved.
>
> **AI prompt to generate a first-pass dossier:**
> Load: `PROJECT.md` + `STYLE_GUIDE.md` + `research/voice-profile.md` +
> `research/terminology.md` + `research/repetition-log.md` + the relevant research file(s).
> Then say: *"Build a chapter dossier for Chapter X using the template in
> `docs/chapter-dossier-template.md`. Flag any research gaps. Do not draft prose yet."*
>
> Author reviews, revises, and approves. Then drafting begins.
