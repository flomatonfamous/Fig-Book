# Personal Content Ingest Rules — [BOOK TITLE]

> **Purpose:** Rules for using personal source material — YouTube transcripts, voice memos,
> recordings, podcast appearances, social media posts — as input for the book without
> treating them as factual sources.
>
> **Core principle:** Personal content supplies voice, stories, and character.
> It does NOT supply facts without verification.

---

## What Personal Content Is Good For

- Capturing how the author actually talks and phrases things (feeds `research/voice-profile.md`)
- Extracting personal stories and experiences (feeds `notes/personal-stories.md`)
- Identifying opinions and positions the author has stated publicly
- Finding specific details that make stories real (names, dates, measurements, places)
- Chapter opening and closing material — personal moments that set a scene

---

## What Personal Content Is NOT Good For

- Technical or scientific claims — these need a separate authoritative source
- Historical claims — verify against reliable historical sources
- Variety or product-specific claims — verify against manufacturer, extension service, etc.
- Statistics or numbers the author cited without source in the recording

---

## Ingest Process

When processing a recording, transcript, or other personal source:

1. **Extract voice samples** — phrases, rhythms, and patterns the author uses. Add to `research/voice-profile.md`.
2. **Extract stories** — personal experiences with enough detail to be useful. Add to `notes/personal-stories.md` with tags.
3. **Flag fact-candidates** — claims that sound factual but need verification. Mark with `[VERIFY]` in notes. Do NOT use in manuscript until confirmed.
4. **Never paste raw transcript text into manuscript.** Always rewrite in the author's voice using the transcript as context, not as copy.

---

## Story Tagging

When logging a story from personal content, include:
- **Tags:** [categories — e.g., climate / humor / failure / technique / community]
- **Best used in:** [which chapter or concept it supports]
- **Status:** Unused / Used in Ch. X
- **Confidence:** Is this definitely the author's own experience, or could it be referencing someone else's story?
